**Core development challenge:** Jhapa holds nearly 60% of Nepal's tea land but the industry is CTC (Crush-Tear-Curl), estate-based, and almost entirely domestic-focused — unlike Ilam's smallholder Orthodox export model. Both districts share underutilized processing capacity across Nepal's 113 tea factories. Meanwhile, the Damak Clean Industrial Park — Nepal's largest industrial park investment, a China-Nepal joint venture spanning 1,422 hectares with 192 factories planned in phase one — is already under construction. The defining question is whether Jhapa builds the local workforce pipeline in time to capture these jobs locally.

## Existing Income Sources

| Source | Details | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **CTC Tea (estate-based)** | Largest tea-growing district in Nepal at 7,195 ha. Avg farm: 0.53 ha/household, productivity 23.05 t/ha, production cost ~Rs 10.23/kg. Dominates domestic chiya culture; exports mostly via India at bulk prices. | Commercial — bulk/domestic |
| **Rice, wheat, maize, betel nut** | Core Terai staples on fertile plains irrigated by the Mechi, Kankai, Ratuwa, and Biring rivers. Betel nut is a notable cash crop. | Commercial |
| **Damak industrial parks** | China-Nepal Friendship Industrial Park (1,422 ha) and Damak Clean Industrial Park (192 factories planned phase 1) — transportation equipment, white goods, textiles/garments, food processing. | Under construction |
| **Cross-border trade & medical tourism** | Kakarbhitta-Panitanki crossing is a major Mechi gateway. Birtamod has 7 eye hospitals (incl. Mechi Eye Hospital) drawing patients daily from Assam and Northeast India. | Informal — high potential |
| **Cement & dairy processing** | Mittal Cement Industries operates OPC and PPC production. Dairy processing supported by dedicated warehouse infrastructure. | Commercial |
| **Cottage & small industry growth** | 776 new small/cottage industries registered in just the first 9 months of FY2024/25 — among the fastest-growing informal industrial bases in Koshi province. | Rapidly growing |

## Market Opportunities

### 🍵 CTC & Herbal Tea
- **Price:** CTC bulk ~Rs 10–15/kg production cost · Herbal blends: premium wellness pricing
- **Domestic:** National chiya culture — guaranteed baseline demand
- **Export:** India (~88% historical bulk share) · Western wellness markets for tulsi/lemongrass/ginger blends
- **Opportunity:** 113 underutilized tea factories nationally (concentrated in Jhapa + Ilam) are ideal for contract-blending herbal teas for Western functional-beverage brands.

### 🏭 Damak Industrial Park Output
- **Scale:** 192 factories phase 1 — transportation equipment, white goods, textiles/garments, food processing
- **Domestic:** National consumer goods market
- **Export:** India via Kakarbhitta · Bangladesh/Bhutan via existing trade routes
- **Opportunity:** Nepal's largest industrial park investment (1,422 ha China-Nepal JV). The binding constraint is skilled local labour, not capital — a vocational pipeline captures the multiplier locally.

### 🏥 Cross-Border Medical Tourism
- **Target:** Structured packages — eye-care + accommodation + transport
- **Domestic:** Eastern Nepal patients also use these facilities
- **Export:** Assam, West Bengal, Northeast India — "reverse" medical tourism, informal demand already exists
- **Opportunity:** 7 eye hospitals already exist organically in Birtamod-Kakarbhitta. Formal branding + hospitality infrastructure converts an informal flow into a structured high-value service export.

### 🌾 Agro-Processing (Rice, Betel Nut, Dairy)
- **Base:** Rice mills and oil extraction already operating (e.g. Sahanshakti Rice Oil Mills) · Dairy processing infrastructure exists
- **Domestic:** Eastern Nepal and Kathmandu valley
- **Export:** India via open border — particularly betel nut and processed dairy
- **Opportunity:** Fertile plains + existing mill infrastructure + FY2082/83 budget's 40% agri-investment support = strong case for cooperative-scale rice and dairy processing expansion.

## Financing Sources

| Source | Type | How to Access |
|---|---|---|
| **China-Nepal Friendship Industrial Park** | International JV | 1,422 ha already committed by Jing Ping Joint Creation Construction (China) and Lhasa ETDZ Investment Co. Local co-investment in workforce training rides this inflow. |
| **CTEVT vocational training expansion** | Government | Factory-floor training co-designed with Damak Industrial Park employers — directly tied to the 192-factory pipeline. |
| **FITTA 2025 — agro-processing FDI** | Legal framework | 100% foreign ownership applies to CTC tea blending and rice/dairy processing — international herbal tea brands can set up directly. |
| **Nepal Infrastructure Bank (NIFRA)** | Project finance | Bhadrapur Airport runway extension and cargo terminal qualify for long-tenor infrastructure finance. |
| **3% Startup Concessional Loans** | Government | Rs 730M pool — tea blending SMEs, agro-processing units, medical tourism support services eligible. |
| **40% agri-investment support (Budget FY2082/83)** | Government | Large-scale rice/dairy/tea processing facilities qualify for up to 40% capital grant support. |
| **Damak Park CSR + local hire mandates** | Industrial commitments | Industrial park employer commitments to local employment targets. |
